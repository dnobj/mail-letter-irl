### Paying for Mail

- There are two ways to pay: **Letter Packs**, which are prepaid letters you buy ahead, and **Pay & Send**, where you pay for one letter or postcard when you send it

- A gift letter from a Letter Pack prints a card for the recipient, with a code they can use once to get a gift letter of their own. A gift letter received from a code prints a card without one. You can't redeem a code printed on your own letter
- A printed code works until the date on the card, 90 days after the letter goes to the printer. A gift letter you have not sent expires 180 days after you receive it
- Gift letters have no cash value. We may end the gift programme at any time

### Sending Letters and Postcards

- Mail is limited to **U.S. addresses**, including U.S. territories and military (APO/FPO) addresses that USPS accepts
- Only you can send mail: you press Send, or pay with Pay & Send. Your AI assistant cannot send it for you
- Letters of two or three pages are printed on both sides. Three pages is the longest letter we print
- You can ask for mail to arrive by a date. We hold it and send it to the printer on the day needed to aim for that date. USPS does not guarantee First-Class delivery dates
- Mail held for a date can be cancelled free until it goes to the printer. Letters paid from your balance come back to it with their original expiry (if that date passed while the mail was held, they come back already expired), and a gift letter comes back with its printed code voided. Mail paid with Pay & Send cannot be cancelled in the app: email support@letterirl.com and a person will decide on a refund
- Once mail has gone to the printer, it cannot be recalled or cancelled
- We check addresses with USPS data. We may correct an address to USPS format and mail to the corrected address. If the check is unavailable, or can't confirm an apartment or suite number, we may mail to the address as you gave it. We cannot guarantee delivery to an incorrect address

### USPS Certified Mail

- While we offer it, letters (but not postcards or gift letters) can be sent as USPS Certified Mail, with or without an electronic return receipt, paid with Pay & Send at the price shown
- Certified mail is signed for at delivery. If no one signs for it or collects it from the Post Office, USPS returns it to the return address on the letter
- We show the USPS tracking number if our printing partner provides one
- Certified mail does not make USPS guarantee a delivery date

## How the Service Works

### Letter Processing

1. You compose your letter or postcard through ChatGPT or another supported AI assistant
2. We show you a preview and what sending it costs
3. Once you send it, the mail is paid for (from your balance, with a gift letter, or with Pay & Send) and queued for printing, or held, if it is timed to arrive by a date
4. Our printing partner (PostGrid) prints and mails it via USPS
5. You can track its status through the service

### Delivery

- Mail is usually handed to USPS within 1-2 business days of going to the printer, and usually arrives in about 1-2 weeks within the US. Mail held for a date goes to the printer on its mail date, unless it is held under "Chargebacks" below
- USPS timing varies and can take longer
- We cannot guarantee specific delivery dates
- A "delivered" status in Letter IRL is always an estimate from our printing partner's schedule. For certified mail, USPS's tracking page shows the carrier's own record
- We hand off mail to USPS - we cannot guarantee final delivery

## Refunds and Disputes

### When We Issue Refunds

- If our system fails to send mail paid from your balance or with a gift letter, the letter is returned to your balance, automatically or once we confirm the mail was not printed. A gift letter comes back with its printed code voided
- If mail paid with Pay & Send cannot be sent before it reaches the printer, the payment is refunded to your card
- If there's a technical error on our end that prevents delivery, we may refund; a person decides

### When We Don't Issue Refunds

- For mail successfully submitted to our printing partner, including Pay & Send mail it has accepted, except where we decide otherwise: email support@letterirl.com
- For incorrect addresses you provided
- For content issues that could have been caught in preview
- For expired unused letter balance
- **All Letter Pack purchases are final** except where required by law

### Discretionary Refunds

- We may, but are not required to, refund the unused portion of a Letter Pack in cash. Requests go to support@letterirl.com with your order id; a person decides.
- A discretionary refund covers whole unused letters only, at the price paid for that pack, and the letters are removed from your balance before the money is returned. It is never given for letters already sent or expired, and it does not change the rule that all purchases are final.

### Chargebacks

If you dispute a charge with your card issuer, we may stop your account from sending mail until the dispute is resolved. Mail that has not gone to the printer is held, or cancelled if the disputed payment was a Pay & Send for that mail. The unused letters of a disputed Letter Pack are removed from your balance. If the dispute is resolved in our favour, those letters are restored with their original expiry and held mail can then be sent.
